# Product Context - Quadra

## Product Overview
Quadra is a specialized accounting reconciliation platform designed for RG90 Conciliation at the accounting/ledger level.

### Product Name
**Quadra** - "Conciliación RG90 — Nivel Contable"

### Purpose
Enable accounting teams to efficiently reconcile financial data at the detailed accounting level according to RG90 standards (regulatory requirement in Argentina for financial institutions).

## Users & Personas

### Primary Users
- **Accounting Professionals**: Team members responsible for daily reconciliation tasks
- **Accounting Managers**: Supervisors overseeing reconciliation workflows and quality
- **Finance Teams**: Supporting accounting operations

### User Goals
1. Quickly identify and resolve reconciliation discrepancies
2. Process reconciliations efficiently with minimal manual work
3. Maintain compliance with RG90 regulatory standards
4. Collaborate with team members on complex reconciliations
5. Track reconciliation history and maintain audit trails

### User Context
- **Expertise Level**: High - professional accountants and finance specialists
- **Time Sensitivity**: High - daily reconciliation deadlines
- **Data Volume**: Medium to Large - processing multiple account reconciliations
- **Frequency of Use**: Daily, often during specific hours (EOD reconciliation)

## Key Features

### Core Features
1. **Search & Filter**: Powerful filtering to find specific transactions and discrepancies
2. **Data Display**: Detailed tabular view of reconciliation items with sortable columns
3. **Results Management**: View and analyze reconciliation results with success/error indicators
4. **History Tracking**: Access previous reconciliation attempts and results
5. **User Authentication**: Secure login with password recovery
6. **Team Collaboration**: Share results and history with team members

## Design Priorities

### High Priority
1. **Clarity** - Information hierarchy must be immediately clear to busy accountants
2. **Efficiency** - Minimize clicks and navigation to find needed information
3. **Accuracy** - Visual feedback for success/error states must be unambiguous
4. **Performance** - Fast data loading and responsiveness for large datasets
5. **Trustworthiness** - Professional appearance reinforces financial data safety

### Medium Priority
1. **Accessibility** - Support various screen sizes and accessibility needs
2. **Consistency** - Uniform experience across different reconciliation views
3. **Feedback** - Clear status messages and progress indicators

### Low Priority
1. **Flashiness** - Avoid trendy animations that distract from data
2. **Complex interactions** - Keep workflows straightforward and intuitive

## Brand Voice & Tone
- Professional yet approachable
- Serious about data accuracy and compliance
- Confident in solving reconciliation challenges
- Transparent and clear communication

## Regulatory Context
- **Compliance**: RG90 standard (Central Bank of Argentina regulation for financial institutions)
- **Audit Trail**: All actions should be traceable for compliance purposes
- **Data Security**: Handle financial data with appropriate security measures

## Design Constraints
1. **Professional Appearance** - Must appear suitable for financial institution use
2. **Data Density** - Need to display significant data without overwhelming users
3. **Accessibility** - Some users may have vision or motor impairments
4. **Performance** - Cannot lag when processing large reconciliation datasets

## Success Metrics
- Quick discrepancy identification time
- High first-time reconciliation completion rate
- User confidence in data accuracy
- Low error rates in reconciliation matching
